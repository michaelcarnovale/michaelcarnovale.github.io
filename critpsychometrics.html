Critical Psychometrics Reading List

A bibliography of over 350 scientific articles, chapters, and books that involve critiques of psychometrics (e.g., conceptual, philosophical, methodological). A .bib file for all the references below can be downloaded here. Organized by year of publication.

If you would like to suggest articles for this list, including important ones I may have missed, please don’t hesitate to !
